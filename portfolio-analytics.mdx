Your profile is the home for everything about your own account: total equity, open positions, working orders, complete trading history, and performance analytics computed from your trades. The same layout powers every trader profile on Hyperdash, so the stats you study on others are the stats you see on yourself.

## Account overview

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Account Value | Total equity, broken down across Perps, Spot, Staked, and Copytrade balances |
| Overview | Unrealized PNL, Account Leverage, Margin Usage, All Time PNL, and Volume |
| Performance | Drawdown, Win Rate, and Sharpe, switchable between 7D, 30D, and All Time |
| Analysis | Longest Win Streak, Trading Style, average and median trade duration, and your PNL and Size cohorts |

Trading Style classifies you by average trade duration: Scalp (under 15 minutes), Intraday (15 minutes to 6 hours), Swing (6 hours to 2 weeks), Position (over 2 weeks), or Algorithmic. Widgets alongside show your long/short direction bias, leverage, and margin usage at a glance.

## PNL views

- **Chart**: toggle between account **Value** and **PNL**, scoped to **Perps** or **Combined**, over 24H, 7D, 30D, or all time.
- **Calendar**: a daily heatmap of profit and loss. Each day is shaded green or red by size of the day's result, with PNL and ROE in the tooltip.
- **PNL Calendar**: a month-by-month view you can page back through your history, including your longest positive streak in days.

## Positions, orders, and history

| Tab | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Positions | Open positions with entry, mark, PNL, and ROE |
| Balances | Spot token balances |
| Orders | Open orders |
| Fills | Recent order executions |
| Trades | Completed trades, filterable by result, side, asset, notional, and size |
| TWAP | Active and filled [TWAP](/orders/twap) orders |
| Transfers | Deposit and withdrawal history |
| Performance | Best Trades and per-asset Asset Performance breakdowns |
| Copytrade | Your copytrade wallets, filtered by Running, Stopped, or Archived |

You can share any position or your PNL as an image card directly from the table.

## Capital allocation

When a copytrade wallet follows more than one trader, the allocation editor distributes its capital across them: an **Equal** split or **Custom** weight sliders per trader. Allocations must add up to 100%, and every tracked trader needs a share above 0%. See [Copy Trading](/copy-trade) for the full setup flow.
