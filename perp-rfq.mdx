Perp RFQ (Request for Quote) brings institutional liquidity on demand to Hyperdash. Instead of working a large order through the visible book, you request liquidity from whitelisted market makers, who respond by adding two-sided liquidity directly to the order book: larger size, improved execution, better pricing.

## How RFQ works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit a request">
    Open the **RFQ** tab in the trade panel, pick your market, and set your size in USDC or in the asset. Choose Buy/Long or Sell/Short. Your direction stays hidden from market makers unless you tick **Disclose side**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Market makers respond">
    Whitelisted market makers receive the market and size of your request and have **5 seconds** to respond or pass. Responding market makers add real two-sided liquidity directly to the public order book.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the book improve">
    Your RFQ ticket tracks the order book as liquidity arrives, showing your updated estimated slippage, average execution price, and the improvement versus the pre-RFQ book.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade, or walk away">
    You have **30 seconds** to execute. Nothing trades until you confirm, and you can cancel the request or let it expire at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key parameters

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Maximum order size | \$500,000 notional per request |
| Execution window | 30 seconds, then the request expires |
| Market maker reply time | 5 seconds to add liquidity or pass |
| Size input | USDC notional or base asset amount |
| Direction | Hidden from market makers by default, optional to disclose side |
| Access | Private beta, invite-only |

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I get access?">
    Perp RFQ is in private beta and invite-only. [Request access](https://hyperdash.com/rfq) and our team will verify your eligibility.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do market makers see?">
    The market and the size of your request. Your trade direction stays hidden unless you enable **Disclose side**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why would I disclose my side?">
    Disclosing lets market makers add liquidity on one side instead of both, which can tighten your pricing. Keeping it hidden avoids signaling your intent. The choice is yours per request.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Am I obligated to trade after requesting?">
    No. The added liquidity sits on the public book. You choose whether to execute, and if you do nothing the request simply expires after 30 seconds.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does RFQ cost extra?">
    No. There is no additional fee for using RFQ. Standard Hyperdash trading fees apply to the executed order.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if no market maker responds?">
    The request expires after 30 seconds and nothing executes. You can submit a new request at any time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
