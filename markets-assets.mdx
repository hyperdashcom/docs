Hyperdash lists crypto perpetuals and spot markets alongside TradFi markets: equities (including pre-IPO and private names), indices and ETFs, commodities, and FX. TradFi assets trade as perpetuals under **xyz markets**, so they run 24/7 with no exchange holidays, weekend gaps, or overnight closes. Every listing gets a public asset page with live data, charts, and trader analytics.

## What Hyperdash lists

| Market type | What's included |
| --- | --- |
| Crypto perps | Crypto perpetual markets, from majors to long-tail listings |
| xyz markets (perps) | Equities and pre-IPO names, indices and ETFs, commodities, and FX pairs |
| Spot | Spot tokens |

Maximum leverage is set per market: each market's ceiling shows next to it in search results, on the asset page trade button, and in the trade panel. Perps and spot are listed separately on purpose: the same ticker can exist in both with different identities, so always check the market type before trading.

## The Markets hub

The Markets page at `hyperdash.com/markets` is the browsing home: your watchlist, TradFi and crypto 24h heatmaps, the hot asset of the week, trending assets, cohort bias, and live trade flow rails showing where top traders are long, which assets are generating the most profits, and which carry the largest open positions. Curated baskets (Magnificent Seven, global indices, commodities, FX majors, AI infrastructure, Korea market pulse) sit alongside theme categories spanning AI, semiconductors, DeFi, memes, RWA, pre-IPO and private, and more. Each category has its own page at `hyperdash.com/markets/<category>` with a grid or table view, sortable by price, 24h change, volume, open interest, and market cap.

## Asset pages

Every market has a page at `hyperdash.com/asset/<ticker>` showing:

- **Chart**: line or candle, on timeframes from 1 day to 1 year plus year to date.
- **At a glance**: price, 24h change, 24h volume, open interest, estimated market cap, funding rate, and active traders.
- **Trade panel**: a perps or spot order form, so you can trade directly from the page. The trade button shows the market's max leverage.
- **Cohort bias**: how profitable and unprofitable [trader cohorts](/cohorts-top-traders-position-changes) are positioned in the market.
- **Key trades**: the market's biggest PNLs and biggest open positions, split into winners and losers.
- **Overview**: fundamentals tailored to the asset class: supply, FDV, and all-time high for crypto; sector, exchange, P/E ratio, and upcoming earnings with add-to-calendar for equities; contract details for commodities; policy rates for FX; constituents for indices.
- **FAQ and related markets** to keep exploring.

## Finding markets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search from anywhere">
    Press **Cmd+K** (Ctrl+K on Windows) or click the header search. Search by ticker, name, or tag ("gold", "semiconductor"), and filter perps by leverage with queries like `>30x` or `<10x`. The same box looks up wallet addresses and slash commands.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by category">
    Inside search, category chips narrow results to New, Pre-IPO, Equities, Indices/ETFs, Commodities, FX, Perps, Spot, OTC, or Watchlist. For a richer browse, use the Markets hub.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a watchlist">
    Star any asset from its page or from search results. Your watchlist appears on the Markets page, as a search filter, and as a widget in your trading layout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The live app is always the source of truth for what's listed: new markets appear in the **Recently listed** section of the Markets hub as they launch.
</Note>
