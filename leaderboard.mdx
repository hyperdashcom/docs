The Leaderboard ranks traders by net realized PnL so you can find who is actually performing, in which markets, and over which horizon. The top three take the podium with gold, silver, and bronze badges, and your own row is pinned with a **You** badge so you always know where you stand.

## Rankings and timeframes

- **Market tabs**: Overall, Equities, Commodities, Majors (BTC, ETH, HYPE, SOL perps), and Alts, plus an asset filter to narrow any tab to a single market.
- **Timeframes**: 24H, 7D, 30D, YTD, and All Time.
- **PnL display**: toggle Net PNL between dollars and percent. Percent mode shows net PnL relative to the trader's account value.

## Reading the table

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Rank | Position in the current market tab and timeframe |
| Trader | Name or address, with quick actions on hover |
| Account Value | Current total equity |
| Net PNL | Net realized PnL for the selected timeframe, in \$ or % |
| Volume | Traded volume |
| Win Rate | Share of winning trades |
| Trades | Trade count |
| Top Assets | Most traded assets by all-time volume |

Numeric columns are sortable.

## Filters and search

Filter rows by Account Value range, Win Rate range, or trading style tag (Algo, Scalp, Intraday, Swing). Search finds traders by name, and pasting any 0x address looks that trader up even if they sit outside the loaded rankings.

## What you can do from here

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a trader profile">
    Click any row to preview the trader's full profile: performance stats, PnL chart and calendar, positions, and trade history, the same view described in [Portfolio and Analytics](/portfolio-analytics).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track, copy, or snoop">
    Hover a trader for quick actions: **Track** adds the wallet to your tracked wallets, **Copy** copies the address, and **Snoop** follows their activity live on your terminal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start copy trading">
    From a trader's profile, the Copy Score section includes a copytrade button that opens the setup flow directly. See [Copy Trading](/copy-trade).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Ranks are calculated from net realized performance, so unrealized PnL on open positions does not move a trader up or down the board.
</Note>
