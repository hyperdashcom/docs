Hyperdash is a global trading platform and data analytics company. Trade perpetuals and spot across crypto, equities, commodities, indices, and FX, with an execution toolkit built for professionals and market data that turns every wallet, position, and liquidation into signal you can act on.

## Trade global markets

Markets run 24/7 with no market holidays. Perpetuals cover crypto majors alongside equities, commodities, indices, and FX, each with its own leverage limits, and spot markets settle real assets to your own wallet. Browse the catalog on [Markets and Asset Pages](/markets-assets), or see [Spot Trading](/spot-trading) and [Perp RFQ](/perp-rfq) for size execution with institutional liquidity.

## Execute like a pro

Every order type has a dedicated reference in [Trading Execution](/orders/overview): market and limit basics, laddered Scale entries, Stop and Trailing Stop protection, Chase orders that follow the book, and Private TWAP and Swarm strategies that work large size with less market impact. [Quick Trade](/quicktrade) puts preset-size buttons one click from any chart.

## Read the market, then trade it

Transparent on-chain data powers [Trader Explore](/wallet-explore), [Cohorts and Top Traders](/cohorts-top-traders-position-changes), the [Leaderboard](/leaderboard), and [Alerts](/alerts-inbox) on every wallet you track. When you find traders worth following, [Copytrading](/copy-trade) mirrors or counters them automatically with risk controls.

## Your keys, your funds

Trading runs through agent keys that can never withdraw, with 2FA on the account and withdrawals signed only by your own wallet. Details in [API Wallets and Security](/agent-wallets-security).

## Get started

Fund your account from any chain, card, or fiat on [Onboarding and Deposits](/onboarding-deposits), and reach the team any time via [Get Help](/get-help).
