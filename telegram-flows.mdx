Hyperdash Flows is our public Telegram channel for real-time market alerts, generated directly from node-level Hyperliquid data. Every alert is computed server-side against per-market baselines and posted the moment it triggers, with links back to the relevant chart, asset page, or wallet on Hyperdash.

**Join the channel: [t.me/hyperdashflows](https://t.me/hyperdashflows)**

The channel is tuned for signal over noise. Expect roughly 10 to 15 posts per day, concentrated in the US session.

## Market tiers

Alert thresholds scale by market tier. The tiers referenced throughout this page:

| Tier | Markets |
| --- | --- |
| Majors | BTC, ETH |
| Large alts | SOL, XRP, DOGE, HYPE |
| HIP3 Markets | Stocks, indices, and commodities |
| Alts | Every other perp market |

## Liquidations

Posts every liquidation with notional value of \$50,000 or more, in real time. Red marks a liquidated long, green a liquidated short.

## TWAP Started

Posts when a new TWAP order begins on a market.

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum notional | \$50M majors, \$25M large alts, \$10M all other markets |
| Survival requirement | The order must remain active for 60 seconds before posting. Orders cancelled within 60 seconds never post |

## OI Surge

Tracks open interest in 5-minute windows and compares each change against the average absolute change of the previous 20 windows (100 minutes).

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger multiple | 8x the rolling average (6x for HIP3 Markets) |
| Minimum move | \$25M majors, \$10M large alts, \$5M HIP3 Markets and alts |
| Activity minimum | The market's rolling average change must exceed \$2M (majors), \$1M (large alts), or \$500K (HIP3/alts), so inactive books cannot alert off a single order |
| Cooldown | One post per market per 30 minutes |

## Volume Surge

Detects abnormal 5-minute volume bursts. Crypto markets are measured against their short-term rolling average; HIP3 Markets are measured against their trailing 24-hour pace, which absorbs the normal open and close volume cycle so only genuinely abnormal sessions post.

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Crypto trigger | 10x the average of the previous 20 windows, with minimum volume of \$150M (majors), \$50M (large alts), or \$25M (alts) |
| HIP3 trigger | 15x the market's average 5-minute volume over the trailing 24 hours, with a \$25M minimum |
| Included data | Each post shows the buy/sell imbalance of the window |
| Cooldown | One post per market per 30 minutes |

## Funding Surge

Each market is measured against its own funding history, so a market that always runs hot cannot alert repeatedly, while a normally calm market alerts on moves that would be routine elsewhere.

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger level | The market's 98th percentile of hourly funding (annualized) over the trailing 30 days, with a 30% APR minimum |
| Onset requirement | Funding must have spent the previous 6 hours below half the trigger level, so only sudden moves post |
| Reset | After posting, the market cannot post again until funding falls back below half its trigger level |
| Eligibility | \$25M+ 24-hour volume, \$5M+ open interest, and at least 7 days of funding history |

## Daily Market Summary

A single digest posted every day at 9:00 AM EST:

- 24-hour volume, liquidation, and open interest totals with day-over-day change
- The latest Hyperdash News headline
- Earnings on tradeable HIP3 equity markets, covering upcoming after-close reports and everything reported since the prior close
- Top 3 gainers and losers by 24-hour price change (markets with \$10M+ open interest)
- Top 3 traders by 24-hour realized PnL and the 3 biggest liquidations
- The highest and lowest funding rates with their deviation from the 7-day average (markets with \$10M+ open interest)
- Positioning of all four trader PnL cohorts with day-over-day change
