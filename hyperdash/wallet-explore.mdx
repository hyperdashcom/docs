Explore is Hyperdash's trader discovery hub. Browse every wallet trading on Hyperliquid markets, filter the universe down to the traders that matter to you, and track them from a single sidebar.

## Explore sections

- **Cohorts**: traders grouped by all-time PnL and by wallet size, with positioning for each group. See [Cohorts, Top Traders and Position Changes](/hyperdash/cohorts-top-traders-position-changes).
- **Copytrading**: the best wallets to copy, ranked by copy score and ROI. See [Copy Trade](/hyperdash/copy-trade).
- **Tagged**: known traders and funds, labeled for convenience.
- **Leaderboard**: top performers, including per-asset leaderboards.
- **Global**: the full trader universe with filters.

## Filtering global traders

| Filter | Description and options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time period | 1d, 7d, 30d, or All | 7d |
| Account value | Minimum and maximum account value in USD | Any |
| PNL | Minimum and maximum net profit and loss in USD | Any |
| Trading style | All, Algo, Scalp, Intraday, Swing, Position | All |
| Copy score | Range from 0 to 100 | 0 to 100 |

Results show each trader's PnL, equity, total trades, win rate, Sharpe ratio, max drawdown, leverage, margin utilization, and traded assets.

## Tracking wallets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Track a wallet">
    Hit **Track** on any trader row in Explore, on the Top Traders table in the terminal, or from a trader profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and file it">
    Give the wallet an alias and save it to a list. Every account starts with a **Main** list, and you can create new lists with a custom name and color.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage your lists">
    Rename wallets, move them between lists, or untrack them at any time. Lists can be shared by link, and a shared list opens read-only until the viewer clones it to their own account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the feed">
    Activity from tracked wallets streams into the **Tracked** feed in the trading terminal and the Recent Activity panel in the Explore sidebar. Filter the feed by list, direction, open or close, and notional size.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Sign in to use tracking: tracked wallets and lists are saved to your Hyperdash account so they follow you across devices.
</Note>

For how tracked wallet activity relates to platform notifications, see [Alerts and Inbox](/hyperdash/alerts-inbox).
