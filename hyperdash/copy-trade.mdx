Copy trading on Hyperdash lets you follow profitable traders and never miss a move: every trade is copied automatically, in real time. You pick who to follow and how much risk to take, and the platform executes for you.

<Note>
  Copy trading runs on [**Autotrade**](/hyperdash/autotrade), Hyperdash's automated execution engine. See that page for wallet setup, funding, risk settings, and how to start, stop, and edit a strategy.
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover traders">
    The Copytraders section of [Explore](/hyperdash/wallet-explore) ranks the Top 100 copyable traders, plus curated tabs like Equities Focused and top BTC, HYPE, and CL traders. Sort by copy score, ROI, equity, or recent activity; each card shows ROI, equity, volume, and last trade time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy or counter">
    From a trader card or profile, choose **Copytrade** to mirror their positions or **Countertrade** to take the opposite side. You can also paste any wallet address directly, and follow up to 10 traders from a single wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure and start">
    Set your allocation, leverage cap, and filters, then fund a dedicated wallet. Full walkthrough on the [Autotrade page](/hyperdash/autotrade).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track performance">
    The Copytrade tab lists your wallets under Running, Stopped, and Archived, with equity, PNL, positions, and per-wallet logs showing every action taken (and trades skipped, with reasons). You can also share a PNL card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What gets copied

Copy trading places real orders from your Autotrade wallet; it is not an alert feed. When a target trader opens or closes a perp position, your wallet mirrors it (or counters it), sized to your allocation and subject to your risk settings: a max position leverage cap, an optional new-positions-only mode, a max worse entry limit, and asset whitelists or blacklists. Spot and staked HYPE can optionally count toward copy sizing.

If you want to watch top-trader positioning without trading it, use [Top Traders and Position Changes](/hyperdash/cohorts-top-traders-position-changes) instead.

## Stopping and funds

Your copy trading capital lives in a wallet dedicated to the strategy. Stopping always closes that wallet's open positions, and a stopped wallet can be resumed later. Archiving a Hyperdash-created wallet closes positions and returns the balance to your primary wallet. Details on custody, deposits, and the 0.05% builder fee are on the [Autotrade page](/hyperdash/autotrade).
