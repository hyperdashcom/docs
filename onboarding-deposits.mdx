## Signing in

You can sign in with:

- Google
- Apple
- Email
- Any EVM compatible wallet

If you sign in with Google, Apple, or email, Hyperdash generates a non-custodial wallet for you, secured by Turnkey, and you deposit into that wallet to trade. Two factor authentication is available on your account once you are signed in.

## Deposits

The deposit modal groups everything under three tabs: **All**, **Crypto**, and **Cash**. Pick a currency and a method, and Hyperdash shows the settlement time and any minimum before you confirm.

### Crypto

- **Crypto**: send from any wallet or exchange. Supported networks are Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, Polygon, BSC, Solana, Bitcoin, Tron, HyperEVM, and Hyperliquid.
- Choose **Deposit Only** to keep the asset you sent, or **Swap to USDC** to have it converted on arrival.
- **Exchanges**: deposit straight from a connected exchange account. Binance, Coinbase, Bybit, KuCoin, Gate, Bitfinex, and BtcTurk are supported.

### Cash

| Method | How it works | Currencies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bank Transfer | A virtual account in your own name, so you can wire in from your bank. Availability depends on your country. | All supported currencies |
| Card | Visa and Mastercard, including Apple Pay and Google Pay. Hyperdash compares live quotes across payment providers so you can pick the best rate. | All supported currencies |
| Revolut Pay | Pay directly from your Revolut balance. | EUR, GBP, PLN, RON, CZK, HUF, CHF |
| Apple Pay and Google Pay | Available on their own and as a card option. | All supported currencies |
| PayPal | Pay from your PayPal balance or a linked account. | EUR |
| CashApp | Pay from your CashApp balance. | USD |
| PIX | Brazil's instant payment rail. | BRL |
| SPEI | Mexico's interbank transfer rail. | MXN |
| BLIK | Poland's mobile payment rail. | PLN |
| UPI | India's instant payment rail. | INR |
| Interac | Canadian bank transfer over Interac. | CAD |

### Supported currencies

Hyperdash accepts deposits in 34 fiat currencies:

AUD, BGN, BRL, CAD, CHF, CLP, COP, CZK, DKK, EUR, GBP, HKD, HUF, IDR, ILS, INR, JPY, KRW, MXN, MYR, NOK, NZD, PEN, PHP, PLN, RON, SEK, SGD, THB, TRY, USD, VND, ZAR, ZMW

Not every method supports every currency. The deposit modal only shows the methods available for the currency you pick, and hides anything unavailable in your country.

<Note>
  Deposits are restricted in sanctioned jurisdictions, and bank transfer coverage is narrower than the other methods. If a method does not appear for you, it is not available for your country or your selected currency.
</Note>

Once a deposit settles you can see the balance in your account and use every Hyperdash feature.

## Importing a Wallet

Existing hyperliquid accounts can be imported and traded on via their API key in just a few clicks:

- Connect Wallet: Connect a wallet with an associated Hyperliquid account.
- Approve Agent: Generate a Hyperliquid API key for trading.
- Approve Builder: Enable trading on Hyperdash.

Create new non-custodial wallets secured by Turnkey. You can easily transfer funds between wallets from your balance section.
