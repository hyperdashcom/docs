Teams running their own Hyperliquid nodes depend on peers for market data. Public gossip peers are capacity-constrained, disconnect without notice, and degrade under load, particularly around network upgrades.

<Note>
  **Direct Sentry Peering is a private, whitelisted connection between your node and Hyperdash sentry infrastructure, colocated with our validator.** One hop, no middle layers.
</Note>

## Who it is for

Teams that already run, or are ready to run, their own non-validating Hyperliquid node and want a stable path to the network:

- Market makers and latency-sensitive trading firms
- Quant and research funds
- Data and analytics providers
- Infrastructure teams building on Hyperliquid

## Peering or a hosted feed

Sentry peering removes the distribution layer entirely. Any hosted feed has to receive, process and rebroadcast data before it reaches you. With direct peering, your node receives blocks over Hyperliquid's native gossip protocol and you decode locally.

Peering suits teams already running their own node. If you would rather not operate one, talk to us about API access instead.

## What you get

- **Blocks and state updates** over Hyperliquid's native gossip protocol
- **Mempool transactions.** Our infrastructure runs `split_client_blocks` end to end, so `mempool_txs` populate on your node
- **Three whitelisted IPs** included, reserved for your nodes rather than shared capacity
- **Further IPs** on request, so you can move from staging to production without re-onboarding

We are never in your execution path. Order submission goes directly to Hyperliquid's API.

## Before you start

You will need a non-validating Hyperliquid node that you operate, on a machine with a static public IP. Hardware, operating system and port requirements are set by Hyperliquid, not by us: see the [Hyperliquid node repository](https://github.com/hyperliquid-dex/node) for current specifications.

Size disk and bandwidth for mempool capture before you enable it. `mempool_txs` is written continuously and is significantly larger than block data alone.

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Whitelist">
    Send us the static IP of your node. We whitelist it to our validator sentry node and return the sentry IP for your region.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure">
    Set our sentry IP as the root entry in your override gossip config, at `~/override_gossip_config.json`:

    ```json
    {
      "root_node_ips": [{ "Ip": "<sentry-ip-we-provide>" }],
      "try_new_peers": false,
      "chain": "Mainnet"
    }
    ```

    Setting `try_new_peers` to `false` keeps your node on the reserved peer instead of drifting back onto public ones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    Restart your node and confirm blocks are applying. You are live on a fast, stable, always-on connection.
  </Step>
</Steps>
